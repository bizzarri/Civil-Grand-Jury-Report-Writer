# Template provenance and attribution

The original application source is distributed under the MIT license in `LICENSE`.

`source/engine/template/revised.docx` is the supplied **CGJA_Report_Template_Accessibility_Revised.docx**, a local accessibility revision of a California Grand Jurors’ Association (CGJA) sample report template. Its package metadata identifies Larry Herbst as the original creator and describes it as adapted from the supplied CGJA sample. The revision is not endorsed by CGJA and is not an accessibility certification. Attribution and the template’s original metadata have been retained.

The repository’s MIT license applies to the original application code and documentation. It does not relicense third-party template material or grant rights to the CGJA name, marks, or underlying sample. Respect the applicable rights and permissions for that material when redistributing or replacing the template. No broader third-party license is asserted here.

Template SHA-256: `00e6f4f313519a6d722ef17e95870ece90fb2b4245fbe3ef2d754d31337ce44b`.

The application shows template instructions as authoring guidance. It excludes that guidance and unused placeholders from exported reports. Publication still requires factual, legal and accessibility review by the responsible author and court.
